Ocarina of Time Randomizer

Shuffles where every item is, opens story lockouts if you want, and builds a patched copy of your own game dump to load in your emulator. Every seed is checked to be beatable.

Where items hide

Chests, Gold Skulltulas, pots and crates, Heart Pieces, Business Scrubs, and shop stock and prices. Switch each one on or off.

Logic or chaos

With logic, every item is reachable with what you found before it. With no logic, anything goes.

Open the world

Start with Kokiri Forest, the Deku Tree, the Door of Time or Zora's Fountain already open. The logic follows.

Item tracker

An overlay on top of the emulator that lights up what you've found. Show or hide it with a controller combo.

Fewer cutscenes

Skip the intro, and make every cutscene skippable, including the three the game normally locks.

Updates itself

The app checks for a new build when it opens, downloads and verifies it, and switches over when you restart.

How it works

Nothing from the game is included or downloaded. The app only reads the dump you made from your own cartridge.

  1. Download and open the app. One file, nothing to install. Windows may ask once whether to run a new app.
  2. Pick your dump. Your own .nup of the game with the 2.0 update. The app checks every file it needs and never changes the original.
  3. Choose what to shuffle and press Forge. You get OoTRando-<seed>.nup, a patched copy of your dump, plus an optional spoiler log.
  4. Load the new .nup in your emulator. It shows up with its own name, "OoT Remake Rando" and the date by default.
